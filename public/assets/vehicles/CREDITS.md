# Vehicle model credits

Some vehicle bodies in COLOMBO: Shadows of the Island are built from third-party 3D models released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

All of them were **modified** by `tools/cars/build.mjs`:
- manufacturer logos, badges, model scripts and the original number plates were removed (badge openings filled
  with plain body-colour blanks);
- the original cabin was removed and replaced by the game's own interior;
- the meshes were re-segmented into the game's materials, the doors cut out on their seams, normals rebuilt,
  and LOD meshes generated.

In the game the vehicles carry fictional make and model names. No endorsement by the original authors or by any
vehicle manufacturer is implied. Vehicle shapes may resemble real production cars; all trademarks belong to their
respective owners and none are used in the game.
